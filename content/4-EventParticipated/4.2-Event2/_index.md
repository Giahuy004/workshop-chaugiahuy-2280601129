# Summary Report: "Study Tour: AWS — Enterprise Cloud Architectures & Industry Applications"

### Event Objectives

- Provide students with a comprehensive, real-world perspective of the cloud computing and Generative AI (GenAI) industry.
- Gain deep insight into how large enterprise organizations design and operate massive scale data architectures.
- Bridge the gap between classroom theory and practical industrial application.
- Establish professional networking opportunities between students and leading AWS consulting partners/experts.

### Key Details & Speakers

- **Collaborating Partners:** AWS Vietnam, Swinburne Vietnam, Renova Cloud, and Cloud Kinetics.
- **Audience:** Over 100 Swinburne students who traveled more than 200 km, starting from 5:00 AM to be present at Bitexco Financial Tower.
- **Speakers:**
  - **Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung** – Head of Solutions Architect in Vietnam & Cambodia, Amazon Web Services
  - **Ms. Nhu Tran** – Enterprise Account Manager at AWS
  - **Mr. Khang Nguyen** – Solutions Architect from Cloud Kinetics (also a Swinburne alumnus)
  - **Mr. Vinh Banh** – Data Engineer from partner Renova

---

### Key Seminar Highlights

#### 1. Job Market Landscape & Recruitment Trends
- Received updates on the latest hiring requirements in the Cloud Computing and Generative AI domain.
- Learned about global career development paths for Vietnamese engineers, focusing on certifications, practical projects, and remote collaboration skills.

#### 2. From Classroom to Production: Becoming a Data Engineer
- Panelists shared transition stories from university graduates to successful data and systems engineers.
- Explored how enterprises optimize their database architectures, ensure high availability, and manage cloud costs in real-world scenarios.

#### 3. Bridging the Gap: "Learning by Doing"
- Discussed methods to transform theoretical academic knowledge into actionable solutions.
- Learned how to build portfolios, contribute to open-source, and participate in case-study workshops to demonstrate capability to future recruiters.

#### 4. Fireside Chat: Inspiring Vietnam's Next Gen of Tech Talent
- Explored mechanisms to support, nurture, and develop the next wave of technological innovators in Vietnam.
- Discussed the role of community, mentorship, and continuous hands-on labs in developing cloud competence.

---

### Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned

- **Problem-Solving Mindset:** While technology stacks evolve constantly, having a strong problem-solving mindset and proactive attitude is the key to remaining relevant.
- **Hands-on Experience:** Hands-on training (doing labs, building solutions) is much more impactful than theoretical knowledge alone.
- **Proactive Attitude:** Taking initiative in networking, asking questions, and seeking out mentors is essential for career progression.

---

### Outcomes & Value Gained

#### Technical Skills & Domain Knowledge
- **Enterprise Architectures:** Learned how major cloud consulting partners (Renova Cloud, Cloud Kinetics) design scalable, secure, and cost-efficient cloud foundations.
- **GenAI Applications:** Gained insight into how Generative AI is being integrated into industry pipelines (data retrieval, automation).
- **Data Engineering Practices:** Understood the day-to-day responsibilities of a data engineer and the lifecycle of data within a large system.

#### Soft Skills & Personal Growth
- **Event Support & Coordination:** Helped facilitate and guide the cohort of over 100 students during their Bitexco office visit, assisting with logistics and networking activities.
- **Professional Networking:** Built connections with AWS Vietnam engineers, Renova Cloud mentors, and fellow tech students.
- **Global Career Planning:** Defined a clearer career path for cloud and data engineering, matching academic goals with industrial standards.

#### Contribution to the Team/Project
- Brought back valuable insights on how enterprise projects are designed and structured, allowing us to align our current internship projects with industry best practices.
- Share career insights and cloud technologies with other team members to inspire collaborative learning.
