# Event Summary "AWS Agentic AI Build Week (AABW) Hackathon & Tech Sharing"

### Purpose of the Event

- Participate in the intensive **Agentic AI** hackathon and workshop series organized by **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**.
- Explore next-generation Artificial Intelligence technologies (GenAI, Multi-Agent Systems, Agentic AI Core) and integrate leading AWS cloud services (Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, Lambda, API Gateway).
- Address real-world business and operational challenges: Automated crowd density monitoring, Solution Architect decision support tools, and corporate strategy transition detection.
- Sharpen practical engineering skills under high time pressure (Hackathon), optimize Cloud infrastructure costs, and pitch solutions to AWS experts.

### Scale & Speakers / Mentors

- **Organizers & Sponsors:** Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam, First Cloud Journey (FCJ/FCAJ), AWS User Group & All Builders Welcome (AABW).
- **Participants:** Cloud engineers, AI developers, and students forming teams for intense building during the *Agentic AI Build Week*.
- **Featured Projects:**
  - **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D (Smart Human-flow Evaluation, Prediction, Hazard Detection, Response, and Dispatch):** Automated crowd monitoring and hazard response system powered by Computer Vision (YOLO + ByteTrack), Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore & Strands Agent.
  - **Solution Architect Professional Native App:** AI Agent that parses requirements, generates enterprise cloud architecture drafts, creates Drawio/AWS diagrams, and estimates AWS costs in `ap-southeast-1`.
  - **Signal Scout:** Early detection system for corporate strategic changes and restructuring signals, leveraging AWS Bedrock, LangFuse, TinyFish, and Apify.

---

### Highlights

#### 1. Building Production Agentic AI Applications on AWS
- **Multi-Agent Systems & AgentCore:** Learn to orchestrate specialized AI agents (Autonomous Monitor, Operator Copilot) for automated monitoring workflows.
- **Integrating Computer Vision with LLMs:** Combine real-time vision tracking (YOLO, ByteTrack) with Amazon Bedrock reasoning to convert camera video into actionable intelligence.
- **Cloud Architecture & Cost Optimization:** Design flexible Serverless architectures (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3) and estimate cloud operational costs accurately.

#### 2. Hackathon Journey: Challenges & Real Memories
- **Technical & Time Pressure:** Overcoming obstacles such as working with AWS Agentic AI tools for the first time, managing inference latency, maintaining live video streams, and rapid code iterations.
- **Overcoming Limits:** Debugging until 3 AM with teammates, maintaining high energy, and dividing responsibilities effectively across design, code, and pitch presentation.
- **Team Bonding:** Learning from AWS mentors, exchanging knowledge with competing teams, and experiencing true teamwork throughout the event.

---

### Key Lessons Learned

- **Showing Up Is Half The Battle:** Stepping up to participate in major hackathons is the most important step to breaking personal boundaries.
- **Finished Prototype Over Grand Unfinished Ideas:** Focus on shipping a fully working core prototype rather than scope-creeping unfinished features.
- **Problem-First Design Mindset:** Technology is a tool; understanding the core problem and delivering real value is key to building outstanding solutions.
- **Community Networking:** Connecting with mentors, industry professionals, and talented peers provides long-term value beyond competition prizes.

---

### Key Results & Achievements

#### Technical Skills & Industry Knowledge
- Mastered integration of Agentic AI services and LLMs on AWS (Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, AgentCore).
- Gained hands-on experience designing scalable cloud architectures with real-world cost controls.
- Enhanced capabilities in real-time computer vision and data processing pipelines.

#### Soft Skills & Personal Growth
- Strengthened teamwork capabilities under extreme time constraints (High-pressure Teamwork).
- Practiced agile project execution, role delegation, and technical solution pitching in front of expert judges.
